# Feed Game — Designer A

## Gameplay

- Player picks the correct food from 3 options
- Correct answer: +10 points, heart stays
- Wrong answer: lose 1 heart
- Game ends when time runs out or hearts are lost

## Assets needed

- Food option images (pumpkin, broccoli, egg, salmon, etc.)
- Option boxes (normal, correct, wrong)
- Thinking bubble, timer box, heart box
- Begin/finish dog illustrations for modals

Drop section-specific assets into `assets/`.
